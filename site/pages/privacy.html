Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This website

This site has no accounts, no forms and no advertising trackers. The calculators run entirely in your browser: the numbers you type are never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Prop Firm Manager

Prop Firm Manager stores your accounts, daily results, fees and payouts only on your computer. Once a day it downloads the public file of rule cards from this site to tell you about rule updates (you can turn this off in Settings); nothing about you or your accounts is sent. Notifications are generated on your computer. When you click "Ask your AI", the text shown is opened in the AI service you choose, under that service's own privacy policy. The license check works as described for Trading Journal AI below.

Trading Journal AI

Your journal is stored only on your own computer. The app contacts Gumroad only to check your license key. When you use the one-click AI review, the app opens the AI service you choose (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) in your browser with your review request; that service processes it under your own account's terms.

Purchases and emails

Purchases and free downloads are handled by Gumroad, which processes your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. If you download a free resource, you may receive a few related emails; every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Social media publishing

Calm Edge uses an automated tool to publish its own videos to its own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest accounts. The tool only accesses those Calm Edge accounts. It does not collect, store or process data about other users.

Data deletion

We don't hold personal data about visitors. To ask about data related to a purchase, reply to your Gumroad receipt email; to remove your journal, delete the app's data folder shown in the app's Settings.

Contact

Reply to any Calm Edge email or Gumroad receipt.
