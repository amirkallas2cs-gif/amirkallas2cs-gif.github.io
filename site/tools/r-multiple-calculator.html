The formula

R = P&L ÷ money at risk  ·  money at risk = |entry − stop| × size × point value

For a long, risk is entry minus stop; for a short, it's stop minus entry. A trade that reaches a target twice as far as the stop is a +2R trade. A full stop-out is −1R. A stop that slips gives you a little worse than −1R, which is exactly what you want your journal to show.
