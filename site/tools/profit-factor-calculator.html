The formulas

Profit factor = (win rate × average win) ÷ (loss rate × average loss)
Expectancy = win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss
Breakeven win rate = average loss ÷ (average win + average loss)

The example values are those of a sample journal with 89 trades: a 51% win rate, an average win of $108.89 and an average loss of $77.76 give a profit factor of about 1.46 (the journal itself shows 1.43: the gap comes from rounding the win rate). That's a positive but modest edge. Read more in Profit factor explained.
